Brand system

Proof is the identity.

The mark is a small, repeatable checkpoint: an object has been inspected, attributed, and moved forward.

Proof mark

Use as the app icon, favicon, sender mark, and compact social identity. Keep it blue on ink, ink on blue, or single-color black/white.

Tinkerbot

Wordmark

Use IBM Plex Sans at 600. Never substitute the mascot for the wordmark or use the mark to signify a verification verdict.

Supporting mascot

Use Tinker only in welcome, education, docs/help, and friendly empty states. It is decorative, never an authority signal.

VoiceDirect, exact, calm. Say what changed, who owns it, and what happens next.
Photography & illustrationUse technical craft, real teams, hardware, diagrams, and product proof—not glossy AI imagery or generic office stock.
Motion120–160ms feedback; 180–240ms spatial transitions. Tinker moves only on real hover and is static with reduced motion.
